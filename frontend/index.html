    How it works

    01

    Enter the details

    Provide the applicant profile, loan terms, and credit history. The loan-to-income ratio is calculated for you.

    02

    Model scores the risk

    Your inputs are sent to the FastAPI service, where the trained ML model returns a default probability.

    A probability of 40% or higher is marked as default risk; anything lower is marked safe.
